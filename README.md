# Secure Web-Based Banking System

**SCMP 401 – Software Engineering Studio** · Milestone 1 · Team Delta

Instructor: Dr. Bestoon Hussien, Department of Computer Engineering

## Vision

We are building a secure web-based banking application that lets authenticated customers manage their money online. Customers sign in with multi-factor authentication, open Current Accounts (with an overdraft facility) or Savings Accounts (which earn monthly interest), check their balances, and make deposits and withdrawals that are processed atomically and recorded in an immutable audit trail. Security, correctness of every transaction, and full traceability from requirements to automated tests guide every decision we make.
